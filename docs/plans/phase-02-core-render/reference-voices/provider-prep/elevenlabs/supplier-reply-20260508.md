Hi Henry,

Thanks for getting back to me.

Before scheduling a call, I would like to share one important note: I am originally from China and Chinese is my native language. I can express the product requirements and legal/commercial concerns much more clearly in written Chinese, and I use translation for English communication. Because these questions involve commercial rights, B2B2C classification, resale/sublicensing boundaries, and post-cancellation usage, I would prefer to keep the first step in writing to avoid any misunderstanding.

A bit more context:

Orpheus Narration is an early-stage SaaS product for long-form educational narration. The intended workflow is that a creator pastes a full educational explainer script once, Orpheus handles backend-owned narration orchestration, and the user downloads only the generated long-form audio. We are not planning to offer public self-serve voice cloning, voice resale, sample packs, model-training datasets, or downloads of the underlying voice, prompt, model, or provider voice ID.

For ElevenLabs specifically, we are evaluating Voice Design as a rights/commercial benchmark and possible fallback voice path. The use we need to validate is platform-controlled prompt-designed voices: Orpheus would create safe voice descriptions that avoid celebrities, public figures, brands, real-person soundalikes, and deceptive impersonation; save approved voices server-side; and expose selected platform voices in our product voice picker only so users can generate narration.

Current volume and timeline:

- Current stage: internal provider and rights evaluation, no public ElevenLabs-powered catalog launch yet.
- Near-term volume: evaluation / closed-beta level only; no committed production volume until the rights and contract path are clear.
- Typical output target: long-form narration projects, initially around the educational explainer use case rather than short social clips.
- Timeline: we are deciding the provider and contract path before exposing any prompt-designed voices to users.

The specific questions we need answered in writing are:

1. May Orpheus offer ElevenLabs Voice Design voices as selectable platform voices inside our SaaS product?
2. Does this use count as B2B2C, resale, sublicensing, marketplace use, or making ElevenLabs services available to end users?
3. Does the paid-subscription commercial-use position for generated content apply to rendered audio generated by Orpheus users using our platform-selected Voice Design voices?
4. Can the hosted voice ID remain callable after cancellation, non-renewal, account closure, or plan downgrade?
5. Are Voice Design voices exclusive to our account, non-exclusive, or potentially similar to voices generated for other customers?
6. Are generated audio outputs allowed for monetized YouTube videos, podcasts, ads, audiobooks, educational courses, and social video?
7. Are standalone sample packs, voice packs, voice-library resale, model-training datasets, or downstream resale of voice access prohibited?
8. Does the restriction on sharing Voice Design / synthetic / AI-generated voices into the ElevenLabs public Voice Library also limit a private Orpheus product voice picker where only Orpheus users can select platform voices for narration generation?
9. Are there AI disclosure, watermark, traceability, or metadata obligations we must pass through to Orpheus users?
10. Is Voice Design currently production/commercial-ready for this use, or does any experimental / beta restriction apply?
11. Which paid plan, API plan, enterprise addendum, DPA, order form, or SLA path would be required for this use?

If a call is still needed after the written clarification, I am open to arranging one, ideally with a clear written agenda and written follow-up notes. If there is a legal, commercial, or partnerships owner who should answer these questions, please feel free to include them.

We are looking for the correct paid or contracted path before generating or exposing any platform voices.

Best,
